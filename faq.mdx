You've got questions — we've got straight answers. This page covers the most common things customers ask before placing their first order with papercutsPROB, from the details of our printing process to shipping timelines, return policies, and everything in between. If you don't find what you're looking for here, feel free to reach out to our support team directly and we'll get back to you promptly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What makes papercutsPROB prop money realistic?">
    papercutsPROB uses high-resolution offset and digital printing techniques to reproduce the fine-line details, accurate color palettes, and surface textures of real currency. We select paper stock specifically for its weight and feel — properties that matter as much on camera as they do in hand. The result is prop money that reads as authentic under studio lighting, on 4K and 8K camera systems, and in close-up photography without any additional preparation on your end.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is prop money legal to buy and use?">
    Yes — buying and using prop money for lawful creative purposes is entirely legal. papercutsPROB manufactures all products in compliance with applicable federal and state laws governing novelty and prop currency. Every product is clearly marked as a prop and bears the required "NOT LEGAL TENDER" designation. You should always review our [Legal Use Guidelines](/compliance/legal-use) to make sure your intended use falls within the permitted categories before ordering.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use prop money in a film or TV production?">
    Absolutely — film and television production is one of the most common and well-established uses for prop money, and it's exactly what papercutsPROB is designed for. Our prop bills and coins are camera-ready straight out of the box and are trusted by indie filmmakers and major studio productions alike. Just ensure your production handles prop currency responsibly on set and in accordance with our [Legal Use Guidelines](/compliance/legal-use).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What denominations are available?">
    papercutsPROB offers prop bills in all major U.S. denominations, including ones, fives, tens, twenties, fifties, and hundreds. Prop coins are available in standard denominations as well. If you need a denomination or currency style that isn't listed in our standard catalog — such as a foreign currency replica or a fictional currency for a fantasy production — our [Custom Orders](/products/custom-orders) service can accommodate that request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer bulk or bundle discounts?">
    Yes — papercutsPROB offers tiered pricing that rewards larger orders with a lower cost per unit. For productions that need a variety of denominations without the hassle of building a custom order, our [Bundle Packs](/products/bundles) are pre-assembled at a discounted price and designed to cover the most common production scenarios. If you have an unusually large order or an ongoing production need, contact our team to discuss a custom volume pricing arrangement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I order custom prop money with a specific design?">
    Yes — our [Custom Orders](/products/custom-orders) service exists precisely for this. Whether you need a specific denomination layout, a fictional currency for a fantasy or sci-fi project, a branded prop bill for a commercial, or a unique finish like aged or distressed currency, our team will work with you from concept to final product. Custom orders typically require a brief consultation and a lead time of several business days depending on complexity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does shipping take?">
    Most papercutsPROB orders are processed and shipped within one to two business days of purchase. From there, delivery time depends on the shipping method you select at checkout. Standard ground shipping typically arrives within three to five business days, while expedited options — including two-day and overnight delivery — are available for time-sensitive productions. You'll receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the packaging discreet?">
    Yes — all papercutsPROB orders ship in plain, professional outer packaging with no prominent branding or descriptions of the contents on the outside. This is intentional: we understand that our customers often work in professional environments like studio mail rooms or production offices where discreet delivery matters. Inside the package, your prop money is carefully packed to arrive flat, undamaged, and ready for immediate use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is your return policy?">
    If your order arrives damaged or if there's an error on our end, papercutsPROB will make it right — either with a replacement or a full refund, depending on your preference. For returns due to a change of mind or ordering error on the customer's side, please contact our support team within seven days of delivery and we'll walk you through your options. Custom orders are generally non-refundable once production has begun, so we encourage you to review all design specifications carefully before approving a custom job.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is prop money different from counterfeit money?">
    The distinction is both legal and physical. Counterfeit money is produced with the intent to deceive and pass as genuine legal tender — that is illegal and carries serious criminal penalties. papercutsPROB prop money, by contrast, is produced exclusively for creative and entertainment purposes, is clearly marked "NOT LEGAL TENDER," and contains visible prop designations that make it impossible to legitimately mistake for real currency. Our products are designed to look realistic on camera and on stage — not to deceive anyone in a real-world transaction.
  </Accordion>
