Placing an order on papercutsPROB is straightforward and takes only a few minutes. Whether you're outfitting a film set, photo shoot, theater production, or any other creative project, our streamlined checkout process gets your prop money on its way quickly and securely.

## Step-by-Step Ordering Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Product Catalog">
    Visit the papercutsPROB product catalog and explore our full range of offerings, including individual prop bills, prop coins, and curated bundles designed for different production scales and budgets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Options">
    On the product page, choose your preferred denomination, quantity, and any available customization options. Product descriptions include dimension details and print quality information to help you find the right fit for your production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Your Cart">
    Once you've configured your selection, click **Add to Cart**. You can continue shopping and add multiple items before proceeding to checkout. Review your cart at any time by clicking the cart icon in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Shipping Address">
    At checkout, enter your full shipping address. Double-check the address carefully — once an order is fulfilled, we're unable to reroute packages in transit. You'll also be able to select your preferred shipping speed at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Payment Method and Complete Checkout">
    Select your preferred payment method — we accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Review your order summary one final time, then click **Place Order** to complete your purchase. See [Payment Methods](/ordering/payment-methods) for a full list of accepted options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Order Confirmation">
    After checkout is complete, you'll receive an email confirmation at the address you provided. This email includes your unique order number and an estimated delivery window based on the shipping method you selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Modifying or Cancelling an Order

If you need to make changes to or cancel your order, you have a **2-hour window** from the time of placement to do so. During that window, log in to your account and navigate to **Order History** to request a cancellation or modification.

After the 2-hour window has passed, your order may have already entered the fulfillment process and changes may not be possible. In that case, please contact our support team as soon as possible — we'll do everything we can to assist, though we cannot guarantee modifications once an order is in processing.

## Order Confirmation

Your confirmation email will include the following details:

- **Order number** — use this as your reference for any support inquiries
- **Itemized list** of products ordered, including denominations and quantities
- **Shipping address** as entered at checkout
- **Payment summary**, including the total charged and the payment method used
- **Estimated delivery date** based on your chosen shipping method
- A link to **track your shipment** once your order has been dispatched

Keep this email in a safe place. If you don't see it in your inbox within a few minutes of ordering, check your spam or junk folder before reaching out to support.

<Note>
  If you have any questions about your order, our team is here to help. Reach us at [support@papercutsprob.com](mailto:support@papercutsprob.com) and we'll respond as quickly as possible.
</Note>
