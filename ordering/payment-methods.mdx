papercutsPROB supports multiple secure payment options so you can check out with confidence using whichever method works best for you. Every transaction is handled through an encrypted, PCI-compliant checkout flow designed to keep your financial information safe.

## Accepted Payment Methods

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credit & Debit Cards" icon="credit-card">
    We accept all major credit and debit cards, including **Visa**, **Mastercard**, **American Express**, and **Discover**. Simply enter your card number, expiration date, and CVV at checkout — no account required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PayPal" icon="paypal">
    Pay using your PayPal balance, linked bank account, or PayPal-connected card. PayPal lets you complete your purchase without entering your card details directly on our site.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apple Pay" icon="apple">
    If you're shopping on a compatible Apple device, you can check out instantly with **Apple Pay**. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode — no manual card entry needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Google Pay" icon="google">
    **Google Pay** is available for customers using compatible Android devices or the Chrome browser. Check out with a single tap using your saved Google payment method.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Payment Security

Your security is our priority. All payments on papercutsPROB are processed through an **SSL-encrypted checkout**, meaning your financial data is protected in transit at all times. We use a trusted third-party payment processor to handle every transaction — papercutsPROB never stores your full card number on our servers.

If you ever see an unexpected charge or have concerns about a transaction, contact us at [support@papercutsprob.com](mailto:support@papercutsprob.com) right away and we'll investigate promptly.

## Billing

When your order is processed, the charge will appear on your bank or credit card statement as **PAPERCUTSPROB**. If you use PayPal, the charge will appear in your PayPal transaction history under the same name.

If you don't recognize a charge or have a billing discrepancy, please reach out to our support team before initiating a dispute — we're happy to clarify or resolve any billing questions quickly.

<Note>
  papercutsPROB does not accept cryptocurrency, wire transfers, checks, or cash payments at this time. All orders must be paid through one of the supported methods listed above.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Paying with PayPal is a great option if you'd prefer not to share your card details directly. PayPal acts as an intermediary, so your card or bank information is never passed along to papercutsPROB.
</Tip>
