papercutsPROB ships every order quickly and carefully in discreet, unmarked packaging — so your prop money arrives promptly and without drawing any unwanted attention. Select the shipping speed that fits your production timeline at checkout, and we'll take care of the rest.

## Shipping Options

| Shipping Method | Estimated Delivery | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Shipping** | 3–5 business days | Domestic (US) |
| **Expedited Shipping** | 1–2 business days | Domestic (US) |
| **Overnight Shipping** | Next business day | Domestic (US) |
| **International Shipping** | 7–14 business days | Select countries |

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your selected method, package weight, and destination. Domestic orders over a qualifying subtotal may be eligible for free standard shipping — any applicable discounts will be applied automatically at checkout.

## Discreet Packaging

We understand that discretion matters. All papercutsPROB orders are shipped in **plain, unmarked outer packaging** — there are no logos, product names, or any other markings on the outside of the box or mailer that indicate the contents. The return address label will display a neutral business name.

This applies to every order, regardless of size or shipping method — you never need to request discreet packaging separately.

## Tracking

Once your order has been picked up by the carrier, you'll receive a **shipping confirmation email** containing your tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's tracking page. You can use this to monitor your package's progress from our facility to your door.

If you haven't received a tracking email within one business day of your expected ship date, check your spam folder first. If it's still not there, contact us at [support@papercutsprob.com](mailto:support@papercutsprob.com) with your order number and we'll look into it right away.

## International Orders

papercutsPROB ships to a number of countries outside the United States. International delivery typically takes **7–14 business days**, though transit times can vary depending on the destination country and local customs processes.

Please be aware that **customs duties, import taxes, and carrier handling fees may apply** to international shipments. These charges are determined by your country's customs authority and are entirely outside our control. You, as the recipient, are responsible for any import fees assessed upon delivery. We recommend checking your country's import regulations for prop or novelty items before placing your order.

If your country is not available as a shipping destination at checkout, unfortunately we are unable to ship there at this time.

<Note>
  Orders placed after **2:00 PM EST** may not be processed until the following business day. Orders placed on weekends or US public holidays will begin processing on the next business day. Please factor this into your delivery estimate when selecting a shipping method.
</Note>
