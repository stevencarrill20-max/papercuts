Your satisfaction is our top priority at papercutsPROB. If something isn't right with your order, we want to make it right. Our returns process is straightforward, and our team is always available to guide you through it.

## Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return and full refund, your items must meet the following conditions:

- The product must be **unused** and in its **original packaging**, exactly as it was received
- The return request must be initiated within **30 days of the delivery date**
- The item must not be a custom or personalized order

**Custom orders are non-refundable.** Because custom prop money is produced specifically to your specifications, we are unable to accept returns or issue refunds on those items once production has begun. If you have questions about a custom order before purchasing, please reach out to us at [support@papercutsprob.com](mailto:support@papercutsprob.com) before placing your order.

## How to Start a Return

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email Our Returns Team">
    Send an email to [returns@papercutsprob.com](mailto:returns@papercutsprob.com) with your **order number** and a brief description of the reason for the return. Please include your name and the email address used at checkout so we can locate your order quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Return Authorization and Shipping Label">
    Our team will review your request and respond within 1–2 business days. If your return is approved, you'll receive a **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number** along with a **prepaid shipping label** to cover the cost of returning the package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repack Your Items">
    Carefully repack the items in their original condition and packaging. Include the RMA number on or inside the package as instructed in your authorization email. Do not use the prepaid label for anything other than the approved return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop Off at a Carrier Location">
    Take the sealed package to the designated carrier drop-off location specified on your prepaid shipping label. Hold onto your drop-off receipt as proof of return shipment — we recommend taking a photo of the receipt for your records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Refund">
    Once we receive and inspect your return, your refund will be issued to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**. You'll receive a confirmation email when the refund has been processed. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take a few additional days for the funds to appear in your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or if you received the wrong items, please contact us at [support@papercutsprob.com](mailto:support@papercutsprob.com) **within 7 days of delivery**. Include your order number and, if possible, a photo of the issue.

In these cases, we will send you a **replacement at no additional charge** — no need to return the original item unless specifically requested. We take product quality seriously and will work quickly to resolve any fulfillment errors on our end.

<Warning>
  **Custom orders and opened bundle packs may not be eligible for return**, even within the 30-day window. Once a bundle pack has been opened, we cannot accept it back as a return. If you're unsure whether a specific product qualifies before purchasing, please contact [support@papercutsprob.com](mailto:support@papercutsprob.com) — we're happy to clarify eligibility before you commit to a purchase.
</Warning>
