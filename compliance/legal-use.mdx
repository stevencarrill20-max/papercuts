Prop money is legal to buy, own, and use for entertainment, educational, and creative purposes in the United States — provided you meet certain requirements established by federal law. papercutsPROB designs every product to comply with those requirements from the moment it leaves our facility. Understanding the legal framework helps you use prop money confidently and responsibly in your productions, classrooms, and creative projects.

## Federal Legal Framework

Under US federal law and associated Treasury Department regulations, it is illegal to manufacture, distribute, or use currency reproductions in ways that could deceive a reasonable person into believing they are genuine. However, prop money used for motion pictures, television, theatrical productions, and similar creative or educational purposes is generally permitted under specific conditions designed to ensure the props cannot be mistaken for real currency:

- **One-sided printing** — A prop bill printed on only one side cannot replicate both faces of a real currency note simultaneously, reducing its potential for deceptive use.
- **Clear "NOT LEGAL TENDER" marking** — Bills must be clearly marked to indicate they are not genuine currency.
- **Size differentiation** — Prop bills must differ meaningfully in size from real US currency (either at least 75% smaller or at least 150% larger than genuine notes) OR carry sufficient design differentiation to make them clearly distinguishable from real currency.
- **Design differentiation** — Additional design elements that make the prop clearly distinct from genuine currency may also satisfy requirements depending on context and use.

These requirements are intended to ensure that prop money cannot be reasonably mistaken for genuine US currency when examined by a reasonable person — while still allowing the creative and entertainment industries to produce visually convincing currency props.

<Note>
  The specific federal statutes and Treasury regulations that apply to prop money can be complex and context-dependent. The conditions described above represent general industry practice, not a complete legal analysis. **If you have specific questions about whether a particular use is lawful — especially for commercial productions, large-scale distribution, or international use — consult a qualified legal professional familiar with US currency law.**
</Note>

## Prohibited Uses

<Warning>
  **Using prop money as real currency is a federal crime.** Attempting to pass prop money as genuine US currency — in any transaction, for any reason — is illegal under federal counterfeiting statutes and can result in serious criminal penalties including fines and imprisonment. This prohibition applies regardless of the denomination, visual quality, or labeling of the prop bills. papercutsPROB products are sold exclusively for lawful entertainment, educational, and creative use. Any other use is strictly prohibited, and papercutsPROB bears no liability for unlawful use of its products by customers.

  **Additional prohibited uses include:**
  - Using prop money in any fraudulent transaction or scheme
  - Altering, defacing, or removing "NOT LEGAL TENDER" markings with intent to deceive
  - Distributing prop money in a manner designed to cause others to believe it is genuine currency
  - Any other deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal use
</Warning>
