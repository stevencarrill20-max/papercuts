Ordering from papercutsPROB is designed to be fast, transparent, and hassle-free — because your production timeline doesn't have room for friction. From the moment you land on our catalog to the moment your prop money arrives on set, the entire experience is streamlined so you can focus on what matters: making great work. Most orders ship within one to two business days, and our secure checkout accepts all major payment methods.

## Ordering in Five Easy Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Catalog">
    Start by exploring our full product lineup. Head to [Prop Bills](/products/prop-bills) for banknotes in every major denomination, [Prop Coins](/products/prop-coins) for realistic metallic currency, [Bundle Packs](/products/bundles) for production-ready assortments, or [Custom Orders](/products/custom-orders) if your project calls for something one-of-a-kind. High-resolution product images and detailed descriptions help you find exactly what you need before you add anything to your cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Denomination and Quantity">
    Once you've found the right product, choose the denomination (or denominations) and the quantity that fits your shoot. Each product page displays per-unit pricing alongside bulk pricing tiers, so you can see exactly what you'll pay as you scale up your order. Not sure how much you need? Our product descriptions include general guidance for common production scenarios.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Cart and Choose Your Shipping Method">
    Add your selections to the cart and proceed to the shipping step. papercutsPROB offers several shipping options — from standard ground delivery to expedited overnight service — so you can match your shipping choice to your production's schedule. Shipping costs are calculated in real time based on your order weight and destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Out Securely">
    Complete your purchase through our fully encrypted checkout. We accept all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and other popular payment methods. Your payment information is never stored on our servers. Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive an email with your order summary and tracking details as soon as your package ships.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Prop Money, Ready for Production">
    Your order arrives in discreet, professional packaging — no flashy branding on the outside that might raise eyebrows at a studio receiving dock. Inside, your prop bills and coins are carefully packed to arrive flat and undamaged, set-ready from the moment you open the box. No prep work required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in Every Order

Every papercutsPROB order is held to the same quality standard, regardless of size. When your package arrives, here's what you can expect to find inside:

- **Premium paper stock** — Bills are printed on paper chosen for its weight, texture, and durability under production conditions.
- **High-resolution print detail** — Fine lines, accurate color grading, and sharp typography that holds up under 4K and macro photography.
- **Clear denomination markings** — Every bill displays its denomination clearly and consistently across the entire print run.
- **"NOT LEGAL TENDER" and prop markings** — All products carry the required designations to ensure full legal compliance right out of the package.

<Tip>
  If you're outfitting a full production — multiple scenes, large cast, or a multi-day shoot — our [Bundle Packs](/products/bundles) are the smartest way to order. You get a pre-curated mix of denominations at a lower cost per unit, and everything arrives in a single shipment so your art department stays organized.
</Tip>
