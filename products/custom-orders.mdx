Not every production runs on standard US currency, and papercutsPROB is built for exactly those moments. Whether your script calls for a fictional $500 bill, a briefcase full of branded corporate cash, a stack of weathered foreign notes, or something that's never been seen in a wallet before, the papercutsPROB custom order service gives you a direct line to fully bespoke prop money designed around your creative brief.

## Custom Options Available

You can customize virtually every aspect of your prop currency. Current options include:

- **Custom denominations** — Order bills in any amount: $500, $1,000, $10,000, fully fictional values, or even zero-denomination blanks for stylized scenes
- **Branded currency** — Add a company logo, a fictional brand name, or a character-specific design to the face of the bill — perfect for branded content, commercials, and narrative productions with in-world currencies
- **Foreign-style currency** — Non-US bill layouts with different proportions, color palettes, and typographic styles for international settings or fictional countries
- **Aged and distressed finish** — Pre-worn notes with realistic creasing, soiling, and edge wear for period productions, post-apocalyptic settings, or any scene where pristine bills would break the illusion
- **Oversized or undersized bills** — Scale your currency up for comedy effect, slapstick gags, or stylized art direction, or scale it down for miniature sets, doll props, and macro photography

If you have a concept that isn't listed here, reach out — the papercutsPROB team works with productions of all sizes and is happy to discuss one-of-a-kind requirements.

## The Custom Order Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a Custom Order Inquiry">
    Start by sending your inquiry to [orders@papercutsprob.com](mailto:orders@papercutsprob.com) or completing the custom order form on our website. Include a brief description of your project and what you need — denomination, quantity, design direction, and timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share Design Requirements and Reference Images">
    Once we confirm your inquiry, you'll send over your full design brief. This includes any logos, color references, typography preferences, layout inspiration, or reference images from the production. The more detail you share at this stage, the closer the first proof will be to your vision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a Proof for Approval">
    The papercutsPROB design team will produce a digital proof of your custom currency and send it to you for review. You'll be able to see both sides of the bill, the denomination placement, any branded elements, and the finish type. Revisions are included — we don't move forward until you're satisfied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and Pay">
    Once you sign off on the proof, you'll complete payment for your order. Custom orders require payment in full before production begins. We'll confirm your shipping address and any special packaging requirements at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production and Shipping">
    Your custom prop money enters production immediately after payment is confirmed. Allow **7–14 business days** for production and standard shipping. Expedited timelines may be available for an additional fee — mention your deadline in your initial inquiry and we'll do our best to accommodate it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A minimum quantity of **100 units** applies to all custom orders. This minimum covers the setup cost of bespoke print production and ensures consistent quality across your full run. If you need fewer than 100 units, contact us at [orders@papercutsprob.com](mailto:orders@papercutsprob.com) to discuss your options.
</Note>
