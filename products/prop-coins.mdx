From the clink of a coin on a diner counter to a magician's sleight-of-hand reveal, prop coins add a layer of tactile authenticity that bills alone can't provide. papercutsPROB prop coins are engineered to look and behave like real currency — weighted to feel right in the hand, sized to match actual denominations, and finished in color-accurate plating that holds up under close-up lenses, stage lighting, and high-resolution photography.

## Available Coin Types

The papercutsPROB coin range covers every standard US coin denomination:

- **Penny** — Copper-tone finish, accurate sizing for register and jar scenes
- **Nickel** — Matte silver finish, solid heft for coin-toss and close-up shots
- **Dime** — The smallest denomination, faithfully reproduced in size and finish
- **Quarter** — The most versatile coin for vending machine scenes, poker pots, and cash trays
- **Half-Dollar** — A larger, heavier coin ideal for dramatic hand-to-hand exchanges
- **Dollar Coin** — Gold-tone finish, perfect for high-value coin scenes and period productions

## Quality Features

Every papercutsPROB prop coin is built to withstand the demands of professional production:

- **Weighted metal-look finish** — Each coin is weighted to approximate the feel of real currency, so they stack, spill, and jingle convincingly without looking or sounding hollow
- **Accurate sizing** — Diameters and thicknesses match their real-world counterparts, ensuring proper fit in registers, coin rolls, and prop wallets
- **Color-accurate plating** — Copper, silver, and gold tones are applied to match the visual character of each denomination, so coins read correctly even in close-up or slow-motion footage
- **Durable construction** — The plating is designed to resist scuffing through repeated handling across long shooting days or multi-night theatrical runs
