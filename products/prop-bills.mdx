## Quality Features

Every papercutsPROB prop bill is crafted to hold up under close-up lenses and stage lighting:

- **Double-sided printing** — Both faces of every note are fully printed with denomination-accurate artwork, so you never have to worry about a reveal of a blank back
- **Accurate color gradients** — Our print process reproduces the tonal shifts and color zones of each denomination faithfully, including the subtle green, blue, and purple hues present across the range
- **Realistic typography** — Denomination numerals, serial number formatting, and all text elements match standard bill layouts in font weight and placement
- **Paper weight (~80gsm)** — Chosen to replicate the feel and drape of real currency, so bills fan, fold, and stack naturally on camera
- **Subtle surface texture** — A light embossed finish adds tactile realism that registers in close-up photography and under raking stage light
