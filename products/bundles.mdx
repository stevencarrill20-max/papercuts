When your production needs more than a single pack of bills, papercutsPROB bundles give you everything in one order at the best possible price. Each bundle tier is designed around real production use cases — from a quick one-day shoot to a multi-week theatrical run — so you get the right quantity, the right denomination mix, and the right extras without having to piece it together yourself.

## Bundle Tiers

### 🎬 Scene Pack
**Contents:** 200 mixed-denomination prop bills (assorted $1 through $100)

The Scene Pack is your entry point for production-scale dressing. With 200 bills covering the full denomination range, you have enough to fill a wallet, dress a register, stack a table, or build a convincing hand-off scene. Ideal for one-off shoots, short films, and photography sessions where a realistic spread of cash is needed but volume stays manageable.

**Best for:** Short films, commercial shoots, editorial photography, single-scene dressing

---

### 🎥 Production Pack
**Contents:** 500 mixed-denomination prop bills + 100 mixed prop coins

The Production Pack steps up for multi-scene shoots and productions where cash appears in several setups. The addition of 100 mixed coins rounds out your currency dressing for registers, countertops, and any scene where coins change hands. Everything arrives sorted by type, ready to deploy on set.

**Best for:** Feature films, music videos, multi-day commercial shoots, recurring theater productions

---

### 🏢 Studio Pack
**Contents:** 1,000 mixed-denomination prop bills + 250 mixed prop coins + branded carrying case

The Studio Pack is papercutsPROB's flagship bundle, built for productions that live with prop money day after day. One thousand bills and 250 coins cover even the most demanding cash-heavy scenes, while the branded carrying case keeps everything organized, protected, and easy to transport between locations. The case is durable enough for repeated use across a full production schedule.

**Best for:** High-budget features, TV series, large theatrical productions, rental houses and prop departments

---

### ✏️ Custom Bundle
**Contents:** Your choice of denomination mix, quantities, and coin types

If none of the standard tiers fit your brief, the Custom Bundle lets you specify exactly what you need — a particular denomination breakdown, a specific bill-to-coin ratio, or a quantity that falls between tiers. Head to the Custom Orders page to submit your requirements and get a quote.

**Best for:** Productions with unique currency needs, large-scale events, specialty prop requirements
